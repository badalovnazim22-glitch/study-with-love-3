# Audio assets

The app streams these ambience recordings from Wikimedia Commons and loops them with the selected volume. An internet connection is required.

- **Rain** — “Rain.ogg”, author ジダネ, Wikimedia Commons. Released into the public domain by the author. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg
- **Cafe** — “Cafe ambiance.ogg”, Marble Toast, Wikimedia Commons. CC0 1.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cafe_ambiance.ogg
- **Nature** — “Birds forest.ogg”, Barracuda1983, Wikimedia Commons. Released into the public domain by the author. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_forest.ogg
- **Library** — “20121112 TU Delft Library, quiet study room - general ambience”, el_mar, Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20121112_TU_Delft_Library,_quiet_study_room_-_general_ambience_-_SoundCloud_-_el_mar.ogg

The library recording is used under CC BY 3.0. Attribution: “Quiet study room ambience”, el_mar, via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0. No changes to the recording are made.
